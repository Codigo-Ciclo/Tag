# Citizen Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose
I believe the community around **Tag** should be truly open for everyone. I am committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all players, modders, and contributors, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, ethnicity, religion, or preferred gaming platform.

This Code of Conduct outlines my expectations for behavior in this repository, as well as the consequences for unacceptable behavior.

## 2. Expected Behavior
* Be welcoming, inclusive, friendly, and patient.
* Be respectful of differing viewpoints and game development experiences.
* Gracefully accept constructive criticism regarding **Tag**.
* Focus on what is best for the health of the game and its community.
* Show empathy towards other community members.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior
The following behaviors are considered harassment and are strictly prohibited within **Tag's** repository, issue tracker, pull requests, and related spaces:
* Violence, threats of violence, or violent language directed against another person.
* Sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic, ableist, or otherwise discriminatory jokes and language.
* Posting or threatening to post other people’s personally identifying information (doxing).
* Personal insults, trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
* Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## 4. Reporting Guidelines
If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it immediately using one of the two native GitHub methods below. Because I am a solo developer, I will personally review every report.

### 🛡️ Option 1: Private Report (Recommended)
If your report contains sensitive information or you do not feel comfortable posting publicly:
1. Click the **Security** tab at the top of this GitHub repository.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability** on the left sidebar.
3. Fill out the form. This report goes **directly and privately** to me and cannot be seen by the public or the offender.

### 🚨 Option 2: Public Issue Tab
If the situation is public and you want to flag it immediately:
1. Go to the **Issues** tab and click **New Issue**.
2. Select the **Code of Conduct Report** template.
3. Submit the issue. I will lock the conversation thread as soon as possible while I review the incident to prevent further escalation.

## 5. Enforcement & Consequences
As the project owner, I am responsible for enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. If a participant engages in unacceptable behavior, I may take any action I deem appropriate, including:
* A private or public warning.
* Temporary or permanent deletion of the offensive comments/issues.
* A temporary freeze on your ability to interact with the repository.
* A permanent ban from the **Tag** repository and organization.

Thank you for honoring this Code of Conduct and helping me keep **Tag** a fun, safe project to build and play!
