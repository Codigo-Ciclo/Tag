# Contributing to Tag

Thank you for your interest in **Tag**! As a solo developer managing this project under **Codigo Ciclo**, player feedback and bug reports are incredibly valuable to me. 

Because **Tag** is a proprietary game with an All Rights Reserved copyright license, I do not accept code pull requests or direct modifications to the source files. However, you can heavily contribute to the game's success by helping me track down bugs!

---

## How to Report Bugs & Issues

If you find a glitch, exploit, or crash while playing, please report it through our **Issues** tab using these quick steps:

1. **Check Existing Issues:** Before opening a new ticket, search the open issues to see if another player has already reported the same bug. 
2. **Use the Correct Template:** Click **New Issue** and choose the template that best fits your report (e.g., Bug Report or Feedback).
3. **Be Descriptive:** 
   * Explain what you were doing in the game when the bug occurred.
   * List the exact steps needed to replicate the issue.
   * If applicable, mention your system specs (OS, graphics card, etc.).

---

## Submitting Feature Requests

Have a cool idea for a new game mechanic, map, or feature? Feel free to open a standard issue thread labeled `enhancement` or `suggestion`. While I cannot implement every idea, I love reading community concepts and brainstorming with players!

---

## Code of Conduct Reminder

All interactions in our issue tracker and community spaces must align with our **Code of Conduct**. Please keep discussions constructive, polite, and respectful.
